# Fitt — AI-Powered Fashion Discovery Platform

> **Your personal AI stylist.** Describe how you want to look — Fitt builds the outfit.

---

## What Is Fitt?

Fitt is an AI-powered fashion discovery platform that acts as a personal stylist for every user. Instead of browsing hundreds of products across dozens of websites, users simply describe how they want to look — and the AI builds a complete, coordinated outfit from real products available online.

Fitt is not a store. It is a **fashion intelligence layer** that sits on top of the internet.

---

## The Problem

Fashion shopping is broken in a specific way that nobody has fixed yet.

Most people don't know *what product* they want. They know how they want to feel, where they're going, how much they want to spend, and roughly the vibe they're after. But every ecommerce platform today expects you to already know what you're looking for — and just search for it by name.

That gap between *"I want to look good"* and *"blue slim-fit chinos size 32"* is exactly where Fitt lives.

---

## The Core Experience

The entire product is built around one interaction: **you describe, the AI delivers.**

A user opens Fitt and sees one thing — a text bar. They type naturally:

> *"I have a job interview at a tech startup next week. Smart but not too corporate. Budget around $150."*

Fitt responds with a complete outfit — top, bottom, shoes, and accessories — pulled from real products at real retailers, with images, prices, and direct purchase links. Not a list of random items. A coordinated look, styled with intention.

From there, the conversation continues:

- *"Make it more casual."* — Fitt adjusts.
- *"Show me something darker."* — Fitt adjusts.
- *"Replace the shoes with something under $50."* — Fitt adjusts.

This back-and-forth is the product. It feels like texting a friend who has perfect taste and knows every store on the internet.

---

## Who It's For

The primary user is a **young adult (18–30)** who cares about how they look but doesn't have the time, money, or expertise to figure it out alone. They:

- Shop at Zara, ASOS, H&M, and Uniqlo
- Follow style accounts on Instagram
- Are completely comfortable talking to an AI
- Are aware enough to want to look good, but overwhelmed by the options

Everything gets built for them first.

## Key Features

### Phase 1 — Launch

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Conversational Style Search** | Describe occasion, mood, budget, or vibe in plain language. Fitt understands the full context, not just keywords. |
| **Outfit-Level Recommendations** | Fitt recommends complete, coordinated *looks* — not disconnected products. Every result is a full outfit sourced from real retailers. |
| **Cross-Retailer Aggregation** | Results pulled simultaneously from Zara, ASOS, H&M, Uniqlo, and Amazon Fashion. Rankings based on relevance and quality — not paid placement. |
| **Conversational Refinement** | Adjust color, price, formality, brand, or individual pieces through natural follow-up. No re-searching. No new filters. Just talk. |
| **Personal Style Profile** | The platform learns preferred colors, brands, budget range, fit, and style identity over time. Recommendations improve with every session. |

### Phase 2

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Digital Wardrobe** | Upload photos of clothes you already own. Fitt recommends new pieces that work with your existing wardrobe. |
| **Visual Search** | Upload a photo of an outfit you like — from Instagram, a movie, anywhere — and Fitt finds similar or identical pieces available to buy now. |

### Phase 3 & Beyond
Virtual try-on, trend forecasting, AI closet planning, and weekly outfit suggestions.

## Business Model

| Revenue Stream | Details |
|---|---|
| **Affiliate Revenue** | Primary engine. Fitt earns a commission on every purchase completed through a retailer link. No upfront cost to users. |
| **Premium Subscription** | Unlocks deeper personalization — saved style profiles, unlimited wardrobe uploads, priority refinement, and exclusive styling sessions. Priced for the target demographic. |
| **Brand Partnerships** | Fashion labels can feature new collections through clearly labeled placements. Never hidden inside organic results. Transparency is non-negotiable. |

---

## Competitive Advantage

> Every existing solution helps users **find clothes**. Fitt helps users **decide what to wear**.

That distinction sounds simple. It is actually everything.

The moat is not the technology. The moat is **trust**. When users believe Fitt is genuinely on their side — not pushing sponsored products, not optimizing for commission, but actually trying to make them look good — that loyalty becomes extremely hard to compete with.

**Trust + Simplicity + Outfit-Level Intelligence** is the combination nobody has built yet.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Fitt starts as a fashion search tool. It evolves into the **default answer to the question every person asks themselves every morning: "What should I wear?"**

The end state is a personal fashion intelligence system that knows your style better than you do — one that helps you build a wardrobe with intention, shop with confidence, and show up to every occasion looking exactly how you want to look.

Fashion is personal. The technology to make it feel that way finally exists. Fitt is how we close that gap.
